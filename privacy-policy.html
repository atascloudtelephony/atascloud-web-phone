ATASCloud Web Phone Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 October 2026

Summary

ATASCloud Web Phone is a Chrome extension provided by ATAS Cloud Sdn Bhd for business calls and SIP messages. It stores your phone settings and communication history in your browser. It connects to ATASCloud for account setup and to your configured phone service for calls and messages. The extension includes no advertising or analytics and does not sell your data.

Signing in

When you sign in, your cloud username and password key are securely sent to ATASCloud to verify your account and set up your phone. The extension does not save your cloud password key or the full setup response. It saves the resulting SIP account settings so your phone can reconnect. ATASCloud may maintain account, activation and security records to provide and protect the service.

Information kept in your browser

The extension saves the following in Chrome local storage:

  • SIP username, password, domain and WebSocket address, for registration and reconnecting.
  • Contact names and phone numbers or SIP addresses, for calling and messaging.
  • Recent call details, including caller information, time, duration and result, for history and callback.
  • Sent and received SIP messages, for viewing conversations.
  • Audio-device selections, theme and click-to-call preferences.

SIP credentials are not separately encrypted by the extension. Protect access to your device and browser profile. The extension does not use Chrome Sync storage or read your operating system’s address book.

Connections to other services

Information is sent only as needed for the communication features:

  • ATASCloud receives your cloud sign-in details for authentication and account setup.
  • Your configured SIP provider receives SIP authentication and signaling information, call destinations and message contents to deliver its service. Calls and messages reach their intended recipients through the negotiated service or peer connection.
  • Google’s public STUN service receives network information, including your public IP address, to help establish WebRTC audio connections. This feature does not send it your passwords, contacts or message contents.

Your organisation and phone provider may process service records under their own privacy policies. The extension does not implement call recording; recording by a phone provider or your organisation is governed by their policies.

Telephone links on websites

The extension handles telephone-link destinations and dial text you explicitly select through its right-click call menu. The selected destination is passed to the side panel to prepare a call. It does not inject an automatic telephone-link handler into websites or collect unrelated page content or browsing history for tracking or analytics.

Microphone and notifications

Microphone permission is needed for live call audio. Device setup may briefly open the microphone to check permission and list available audio devices, then release it. The extension does not save microphone recordings. Incoming-call and message notifications are displayed by Chrome and may show caller information or message text to anyone who can see your notifications. You can manage microphone and notification permissions through browser and system settings.

Removing your information

Use Settings → Disconnect and erase data to remove extension-managed local settings, contacts, messages and history. Removing the extension also removes its local storage. Recent call history is limited to 100 records; contacts and messages remain until erased.

Deleting local data does not delete records held by ATASCloud, your organisation, your phone provider or message recipients. ATASCloud keeps service records for as long as needed to provide its services and meet business or legal obligations, as described in its general privacy policy. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information under our control. Browser permissions and system notification records are managed separately.

Security and limited use

Account setup uses HTTPS, new phone setup requires secure WebSocket connectivity, and call audio uses WebRTC media protection. User information is used to provide and support the disclosed communication features.

ATASCloud Web Phone’s use of user information adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is not sold, used for personalised advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Call-center agent status (v1.2.0)

For eligible accounts, ATASCloud verifies the assigned user, tenant, extension and call-center agent and provides authenticated status controls. The extension sends requests to read or change your own agent availability through ATASCloud's service to your PBX. No PBX administrator or adapter service credentials are stored in the extension.

An agent access token is kept in Chrome session storage and a rotating refresh token is saved locally for reconnects. Access tokens last up to 15 minutes; refresh sessions expire after 30 days and may be revoked earlier. Server session records contain token hashes, account scope and expiry information. Status-change audit records include tenant, extension and requested status under the company's service-record retention policy. Expired and revoked session records are removed through scheduled maintenance.

Closing the panel does not change your last PBX agent status. Agent Log off stops queue availability without erasing phone data or ending SIP registration. Disconnect and erase data attempts to revoke the agent session and clears local tokens; removal while offline or uninstalling cannot guarantee a server revocation request. Server expiry limits remaining session validity. Contact lohkianmin@atascloud.com for server-held data requests.
